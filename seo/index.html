The dip is real — but not what it looks like

28-day impressions slid from ~23,600 in early September to 18,945 by early October. The cause isn't lost rankings (position actually improved) — it's August's one-off spike normalising, plus the pause in LinkedIn sharing. Indexing is healthy; the job now is ranking.
