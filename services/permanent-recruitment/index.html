What we do

Hiring for fit, not just the checklist

A permanent hire is a long-term decision, so we treat it like one. Permanent recruitment with JTE isn’t about forwarding a stack of CVs and hoping one sticks — it’s a proper search. It starts with the brief: we take time to understand your team, how it actually works, the problem the role needs to solve, and the kind of person who’ll still be thriving there in two or three years.

From there we map the market. We tap a network built since 2014, headhunt directly, and reach the people who aren’t scrolling job boards — then screen everyone for skills and for fit and longevity, because a hire who leaves in six months costs far more than the salary. You meet a short, considered shortlist of people we’d genuinely vouch for, not a pile to sift through yourself.

It all runs on a success-fee basis — you only pay when your hire actually starts — and every placement is backed by a replacement guarantee whose length scales with the seniority of the role. We stay close from the first interview to the first day, so the person who says yes is the person who stays.

Which do you need?

Permanent vs contract — which fits?

Not every role should be a permanent headcount. Here’s a straight read on when each makes sense — and we’ll tell you honestly if a role is better filled the other way.

Hire permanent when…

  • The role is core to how the business runs and you need it for the long term.

  • You want someone to build knowledge, own relationships and grow with the team.

  • Cultural fit and retention matter as much as the skills on paper.

  • You’re hiring leadership, or a specialist whose absence would hurt.

That’s the search this page is about — success-fee, replacement-guaranteed permanent hiring.

Consider contract when…

  • The need is tied to a project, a peak season or covering a leave gap.

  • You want the headcount without the permanent employment overhead.

  • You’d like JTE to be the employer of record — CPF, leave, IR8A and insurance handled — while you direct the work.

  • You want to trial a capability before committing to a permanent role.

How it works

The permanent search, step by step

A proper search, not a CV blast. Here’s how a permanent hire runs with JTE.

01

Take the brief

We sit down with you to understand the team, the role, the reporting line and what “good” really looks like — plus the salary band, must-haves and nice-to-haves. This is where we’ll also give you an honest read on the market and a realistic timeline.

02

Search & shortlist

We work our network, headhunt directly and reach passive candidates, then screen for skills, fit and longevity — checking track record, motivations, notice period and salary expectations. You receive a short, considered shortlist of people we’d vouch for, each with our honest take.

03

Interview support

We coordinate interviews, prep both sides, and gather candid feedback after each round — so you keep momentum and the strong candidates stay warm rather than drifting to a competing offer.

04

Offer & negotiation

When you’ve found the one, we help structure an offer that lands — managing salary, counter-offers, notice periods and start dates so the deal closes cleanly and expectations are set on both sides.

05

Onboarding & guarantee

We stay close through resignation, notice and the first weeks on the job — the period when a hire is most at risk of wobbling — and every placement is backed by a replacement guarantee whose length scales with the seniority of the role.

A realistic word on timing

A frontline or specialist search often produces a shortlist within a week or two; mid-management and technical specialists usually take longer; and a leadership or particularly niche hire can run several weeks or more — especially once notice periods are factored in. We’d rather quote you an honest timeline at the brief than an optimistic one we can’t keep.

Questions

Common questions

What types of permanent roles do you fill?–

From frontline and specialist roles to leadership hires, across engineering, logistics, manufacturing, healthcare and the commercial and IT functions that support them. That spans individual contributors, mid-management, technical specialists and executive search.

How long does a permanent search take?+

It depends on the seniority and how niche the skills are. A frontline or specialist search often moves in a few weeks; a leadership or hard-to-find technical hire can take longer. After we take the brief, we give you a realistic timeline upfront rather than an optimistic one.

How do you charge for a permanent placement?+

Permanent placements work on a success-fee basis — you only pay when your hire actually starts. There’s no upfront cost and no fee if you don’t hire. We agree the fee and terms clearly with you before any search begins. Get in touch for a quote.

What does the replacement guarantee cover?+

Every permanent placement comes with a replacement guarantee: if the hire leaves or doesn’t work out within the agreed period, we find a replacement. The length of the guarantee scales with the seniority of the role, and we set it out with you before the search starts.

Do you offer contingent or exclusive search?+

Both. Most roles run on a contingent basis — you pay only on a successful hire. For senior, confidential or hard-to-fill mandates, an exclusive or retained search gives the role our fullest priority and a deeper market map. We’ll advise which fits your role.

How do you source and screen candidates?+

We combine our own network built since 2014 with direct headhunting and referrals — including passive candidates who aren’t applying anywhere. Every shortlisted person is screened for skills and for fit and longevity: we check track record, motivations, notice period and salary expectations before you ever meet them.

Can you help with Employment Pass or relocation for a permanent hire?+

Yes. For a permanent hire from overseas we can guide Employment Pass and S Pass applications, relocation and onboarding, and handle ongoing payroll if you need it — see payroll & work pass — so a cross-border permanent hire stays compliant and simple.

Which sectors do you cover?+

We recruit across engineering, logistics and supply chain, manufacturing, healthcare, and the commercial, support and IT functions that keep those businesses running — junior to leadership.
