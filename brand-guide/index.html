01 · Brand essence

Quiet luxury, human warmth

JTE is a premium Singapore recruitment partner that feels calm, considered and genuinely human — never loud or corporate. We serve employers, and we carry ourselves like a trusted advisor, not a job board.

Our mission
“We help employers hire great people, properly.”

Personality — three words

  • ●Human — real people, real relationships; warmth over process
  • ●Refined — quiet-luxury look, calm confidence, never flashy
  • ●Trusted — licensed, discreet, a decade of doing it properly

What we are / aren’t

  • ✓An advisor to employers who need to hire well
  • ✓Human-led, technology-assisted
  • ✗Not a job board that courts job seekers
  • ✗Not corporate, salesy, or loud
02 · Logo

The wordmark

A text wordmark: JTE in Playfair Display (semibold), with RECRUIT tracked wide beneath. Finalised treatment: centred (JTE centred over RECRUIT), the standard gap, and leading-none — used identically on every page.

  • ✗Don’t add effects, boxes, or change the JTE↔Recruit gap

05 · Buttons & interactive

Controls

Gold, black-text primary for the main action; a quiet outline for secondary; the gold pill for “Verify a Number”. Uppercase, tracked, small.

06 · Components & layout

Building blocks

Card + gold accent line

Where you stand

White card, soft shadow, hairline border, and a short gold accent line above the title — the signature motif.

Structure

  • ●Nav: transparent over hero, JTE logo left, uppercase links, always-visible gold Verify pill.
  • ●Footer: coffee #241B12, logo + tagline + columns + “© … EA 14C7215”.
  • ●Sections: generous py-24/32, hairline dividers, eyebrow → H2 → content.
  • ●Texture: a 3% film-grain overlay on dark scenes — subtle warmth.
07 · Photography & icons

Imagery

Warm, cinematic photography of real people & workplaces, always under a signature sepia grade so every image feels part of the brand. Icons are Lucide, thin stroke, used sparingly.

08 · Voice & messaging

How we speak

Warm, plain, confident. Short sentences. We talk to employers, like a trusted advisor — helpful, never pushy, never gimmicky.

Do

  • ✓“Great People, Great Work.” — the tagline
  • ✓Speak to employers: “hire well”, “the right person”
  • ✓Human, specific, reassuring; lead with value
  • ✓Cite the licence & a decade of experience for trust

Don’t

  • ✗Never invite or address job seekers as customers
  • ✗No hype, clichés (“pay peanuts…”), or hard sell
  • ✗No jargon or corporate filler
  • ✗Never overclaim — stay honest, add disclaimers where due
