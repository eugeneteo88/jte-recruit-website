What We Do

Services that actually work

From your first hire to a fully outsourced team — practical recruitment services for Singapore employers, delivered with a human touch.

01 · Permanent

Permanent Hiring

We place people who fit your team and stay — not just CVs that match a job description. From frontline hires to specialists and leadership.

  • A proper search, not a stack of forwarded CVs
  • Screened for skills and culture fit
  • Replacement guarantee on every placement

02 · Flexible

Temp & Contract

Need someone yesterday? Pre-vetted professionals ready to step in for cover, projects and busy periods — same quality, no long-term commitment.

  • Pre-vetted people, often mobilised within days
  • For projects, seasonal peaks and cover
  • Scale up and down as the work changes

03 · Fully managed

Manpower Outsourcing

A full workforce without the overhead. We recruit, manage and pay your team while you focus on running and growing the business.

  • We recruit, supervise and payroll the team
  • CPF, leave, claims and work passes handled
  • Built for warehouse, operations and logistics floors

04 · Compliance

Payroll & Work Pass

Payday should be simple. We handle the calculations, CPF, filings and work pass applications — your people get paid right and on time.

  • Accurate payroll, CPF and IR8A filings
  • Work pass applications and renewals
  • Tax clearance and compliance covered

05 · At scale

Project & Mass Hiring

Opening a facility or launching a division? High-volume hiring that doesn’t sacrifice quality — mobilising quickly to fill 10 roles or 100, end to end.

  • Mobilise fast when you need scale
  • Screening and coordination at volume
  • One team running the whole hiring drive

Employer Branding

Great talent has options. We help you define and show what makes your company worth joining — so the right people are excited before they even apply.
