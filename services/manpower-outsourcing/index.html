What we do

Your team, managed end to end

Building and running a workforce is a job in itself — recruiting, payroll, CPF, leave, compliance, the daily people admin. For many companies, that’s a distraction from the actual business.

With manpower outsourcing, we take it on. We source the people, put them on our payroll, and manage the administration and compliance — you direct the work, we handle the rest. It’s how you scale without building a whole HR function.

Questions

Common questions

How is this different from permanent hiring?–

With permanent hiring, the employee joins your payroll. With outsourcing, they sit on ours — we manage employment, payroll and compliance while you direct their work.

Can we scale the team up and down?+

Yes — that flexibility is the point. We adjust headcount with your demand without you carrying the fixed overhead.

Who handles compliance and payroll?+

We do — CPF, leave, claims, work passes where needed, and MOM compliance throughout.

What kinds of roles suit outsourcing?+

Operational and support roles where you need reliable capacity without building the HR machinery around it.

Want to scale without the overhead?

Tell us the roles and the numbers. We’ll show you how outsourcing could work for you.
