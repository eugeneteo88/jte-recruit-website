Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Does JTE Recruit charge job seekers any fees?+

No. Legitimate recruitment is never charged to candidates. JTE is paid by the employers who engage us, never by individuals, so anyone asking you to pay is not a genuine recruiter.

How can I verify that a JTE consultant is genuine?+

Enter the WhatsApp number on our verification page to confirm it belongs to a real JTE consultant. JTE will never ask you to pay a deposit, transfer money, or complete paid tasks. Anyone who does that is running a scam.

What services does JTE Recruit offer?+

Permanent hiring, temporary and contract staffing, manpower outsourcing, payroll and work pass management, project and mass hiring, and employer branding.

Where is JTE Recruit located?+

We're at 1 Gateway Drive, #10-14 Westgate Tower, Singapore 608531.

Is JTE Recruit a licensed employment agency?+

Yes. JTE Recruit Pte Ltd holds Employment Agency Licence No. 14C7215, issued by Singapore's Ministry of Manpower.

How do I apply for a job through JTE Recruit?+

JTE Recruit is engaged by employers to fill specific roles, rather than helping individuals search for jobs. If you've applied to a role we're managing, the consultant handling it will contact you directly. Please note we don't accept CVs or applications sent to us over WhatsApp.

How can employers engage JTE for hiring?+

Contact us through the form or email for a no-obligation conversation. We handle permanent, contract, and project or mass hiring, as well as outsourced manpower.

Does JTE handle work passes and payroll for foreign employees?+

Yes. Work pass application and payroll administration are among our services, so we can manage the paperwork for foreign hires.

Which areas does JTE Recruit serve?+

We're based at Westgate Tower in Jurong East and work with employers across Singapore.

What industries does JTE Recruit specialise in?+

We have particular strength in engineering, logistics, manufacturing, and healthcare recruitment, and also place talent across other sectors in Singapore.
