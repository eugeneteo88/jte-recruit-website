What we do

Make people want to work with you

When you’re hiring, you’re not just assessing candidates — they’re assessing you. In Singapore’s tight labour market, the strongest people almost always have more than one option, and they do their homework: they read your careers page, look you up on LinkedIn, ask around, and form a view of your company long before they hit “apply”. The employers who consistently win talent are the ones who’ve made that view a clear and honest one.

That reputation as a place to work is your employer brand. It exists whether or not you’ve ever thought about it — the only question is whether it’s telling your story accurately, or leaving good candidates to guess. Employer branding is simply the work of taking control of it: getting clear on what genuinely makes your company worth joining, saying it in language real people believe, and making sure the experience of applying and interviewing lives up to the promise.

We help you find that story and tell it well — from your value proposition to your careers content to the way a candidate is treated from first contact to offer — so the right people arrive already interested, and every role you open with us gets easier to fill. We’ve been hiring for Singapore employers since 2014, so this isn’t branding theory: it’s built on what we see actually move candidates to say yes.

Why it matters

A tight market rewards the employers people trust

Singapore employers are hiring into one of the tightest talent markets in the region. Skilled candidates in engineering, healthcare, tech and the trades are courted by several companies at once, and pay alone rarely wins them — the deciding factor is often whether they can picture themselves happy and growing with you. If your reputation as an employer is vague, they hesitate; if it’s clear and appealing, they lean in.

A weak or absent employer brand quietly taxes everything you do to hire. Adverts get fewer and weaker responses. Recruiters spend longer selling a role that should sell itself. Offers get turned down late in the process because a candidate never quite understood why you were the better choice. And the people who do join without a clear picture are the ones most likely to leave within the year — the most expensive kind of hire there is.

A strong employer brand does the opposite: it works for you around the clock. It draws in people who already suit how you work, gives your best staff a reason to stay and refer their friends, and shortens every search because candidates arrive warm. It doesn’t need to be loud or expensive — it needs to be true, clear and consistently shown where candidates are actually looking.

Questions

Common questions

What is employer branding, in plain terms?–

Your employer brand is your reputation as a place to work — what people believe about you before they apply, and what they experience once they do. Employer branding is simply the work of making that reputation clear, honest and consistent, from your careers content to how a candidate is treated in an interview.

Is employer branding only for big companies?+

No. Smaller and mid-sized companies often benefit most. You may not have the name recognition of a multinational, but a clear, genuine story about who you are and what it’s like to work with you helps you compete for the same talent — often on things money alone can’t buy.

Does employer branding actually help hiring?+

Yes. When candidates already understand and like what you stand for, roles fill faster, offers get accepted more often, and you attract better-fit people who stay longer. It also filters out those who were never going to suit you, so you spend less time on the wrong interviews.

What is an EVP, and do we need one?+

An employee value proposition, or EVP, is the honest answer to “why would a good person choose to work here, and stay?” It covers the work, the people, growth, flexibility, stability and pay. You don’t need a glossy campaign, but you do need clarity — because a clear EVP is what every advert, careers page and recruiter conversation draws from.

Do you write the content for us?+

We can help shape your careers page, job adverts and hiring messaging, and guide your social presence — as much or as little as you need. Some employers want us to draft everything; others just want a sharper brief and an honest outside read. We work either way.

How long does it take to see a difference?+

Some things change quickly — a rewritten job advert or a tidied-up careers page can lift response within weeks. A fuller shift in reputation and candidate experience builds over months as more people move through a better process and talk about it. We focus on the quick, practical wins first.

How does this fit with your recruitment?+

Naturally — a stronger employer brand makes every search we run for you easier and more effective. Candidates arrive already warm, and our recruiters have a real story to tell rather than a generic pitch. See how it feeds our permanent and contract hiring.

Which sectors do you work with?+

We support employers across engineering, logistics and supply chain, manufacturing, healthcare, IT and tech, and the commercial and support functions that keep those businesses running — from a single specialist role to hiring a whole team.
