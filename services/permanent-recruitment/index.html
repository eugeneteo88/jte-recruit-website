What we do

Hiring for fit, not just the checklist

A permanent hire is a long-term decision, so we treat it like one. We don’t just send you CVs — we send you people who fit. Before a search begins, we take time to understand your team, how it works, and the kind of person who’ll thrive in it.

Then we go and find them. From the first interview to the first day, we stay close — so the person who says yes is the person who stays.

Questions

Common questions

What types of permanent roles do you fill?–

From frontline and specialist roles to leadership hires, across engineering, logistics, manufacturing, healthcare and the commercial functions that support them.

How long does a permanent search take?+

It depends on the role, but we move quickly without cutting corners. After we understand the brief, we’ll give you a realistic timeline upfront.

How do you charge?+

Permanent placements work on a success-fee basis — you only pay when you hire. We’ll walk you through the terms clearly before any search begins. Get in touch for a quote.

Do you guarantee your placements?+

Yes. Permanent placements come with a replacement guarantee. The length depends on the seniority and type of role, and we agree it with you before the search starts.

Looking to make a permanent hire?

Tell us the role and the kind of person you’re after. No obligations — just a straight conversation.
