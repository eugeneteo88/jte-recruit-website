Why I do it properly

Getting payroll wrong here is no joke

Payroll rules in Singapore are strict, and the penalties are real. Pay CPF late or short and you’re looking at interest and enforcement. Miss an IR21 and your leaving staff’s final pay gets held up. Keep someone on the wrong or an expired pass and that’s an offence — fines and debarment, not just a warning.

That’s the quiet risk sitting inside every payroll run — and it’s exactly what I take off your hands. We’ve been doing this since 2014, so keeping up with MOM, CPF and IRAS is just how we work. You get it done right without having to become the expert yourself.
