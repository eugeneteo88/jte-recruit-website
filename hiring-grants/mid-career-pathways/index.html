How it works

Meet the person before you make it permanent

Some of the best hires are experienced people moving into something new — but a CV can’t always prove the fit, and committing to a permanent hire feels like a leap. MCP removes that risk. You take the person on a structured 4–6 month attachment, the government co-funds most of their allowance, and you both find out whether it works before anyone signs a permanent contract.

Our part is the hard half: we find you a capable mid-career professional worth the attachment, and help put together the development plan and application so it goes through cleanly. If it works out — and it usually does when the match is right — you hire them permanently.

Common questions

MCP, answered plainly

How much of the allowance does MCP fund?–

The government co-funds up to 70% of the trainee’s monthly attachment allowance, which can be up to $3,800/month. You cover the remaining 30% during the 4–6 month attachment.

Do I have to hire the person after the attachment?+

Yes — MCP is designed as try-before-you-hire. If the individual’s training and performance are satisfactory during the attachment, you’re expected to hire them on a permanent basis. That’s the point of the programme.

Who is eligible?+

The individual must be a Singapore Citizen or PR aged 40 and above. Your company must be legally registered in Singapore and able to offer a structured 4–6 month attachment with a development plan.

How is MCP different from CCP?+

With CCP you hire the person first, then reskill them with salary support. With MCP you take them on a funded attachment to try them out, and hire after if it works. MCP suits experienced 40+ talent and a try-before-you-commit approach.

What does it cost to work with JTE on this?+

We source and vet the mature candidate and help with the application. Recruiting works on a success-fee basis, agreed clearly upfront, and the MCP co-funding offsets a large part of the attachment allowance. See all hiring grants.
