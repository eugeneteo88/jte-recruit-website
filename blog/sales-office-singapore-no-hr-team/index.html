Setting up a sales office in Singapore? You don't need an HR team first

Opening a small office in Singapore doesn't mean building a back-office first. Here's how overseas companies hire their first people here without the overhead.

Eugene Teo

Co-Founder, JTE Recruit · 27 October 2026 · 6 min read

A lot of overseas companies come to Singapore the same way: open a small sales or support office, put two or three people on the ground, see how it goes. Sensible. But somewhere in the planning, someone says 'we'll need HR and payroll set up', and suddenly a lean little office looks like a big commitment.

You don't actually need all that to start. Here's the leaner way I'd do it.

The office is small. The back-office shouldn't be big.

When you're testing a new market, the whole point is to stay light. Two or three salespeople, maybe a support person. But paying those people properly in Singapore still means CPF, payslips, the right passes, the filings, the full set, even for three staff.

So companies feel forced to either set up a whole payroll function for a tiny team, or get their head office overseas to try to run Singapore payroll from afar, which rarely goes smoothly.

Running Singapore payroll from overseas is harder than it looks

I've seen plenty of head offices try to process Singapore payroll from another country. CPF rules, the pass requirements, local filing deadlines, it's a lot of specific local detail to get right from the outside, and the penalties for getting it wrong sit with the Singapore side.

It's not that your overseas team isn't capable. It's that Singapore has its own rules, and learning them properly for three staff is a poor use of anyone's time.

You don't need your own entity to put people on the ground

Here's the part a lot of companies don't realise: you can have people working for you in Singapore without setting up your own company here first. They go onto our company as the employer of record. We handle their payroll, CPF, passes and filings locally; they do your work.

That means you can test the market with real people on the ground, properly employed and paid, without the cost and delay of incorporating and building a back-office.

What this frees you up to do

With the payroll and compliance carried for you, you get to focus on the only thing that matters when you're entering a market: whether the business works here. Your people sell and support; we handle the admin behind them.

And if the market proves out and you decide to set up your own entity later, you do it from a position of knowledge, not a guess.

And it costs almost nothing to start

The bit that makes this easy: it's near-zero cost. For most employers we run payroll free for the first year. For a company still deciding whether Singapore is worth the commitment, that's about as low-risk an entry as you'll find.

Getting set up is quick too, so you can have people working sooner rather than waiting months on incorporation.

Worth a quick chat before you commit to anything

If you're weighing up a Singapore office, talk to us before you commit to setting up an entity or building a payroll function. No obligation, just a straight conversation about the lightest way to get people on the ground.

The takeaway You can test the Singapore market with real, properly-employed people on the ground, without building your own entity or HR team first.

Frequently asked questions

Can we employ people in Singapore without our own company here?

Yes. They go onto our company as the employer of record, properly employed, CPF and all, while they do your work. It's how a lot of overseas firms get started here.

Why not just run payroll from our head office overseas?

You can try, but Singapore's CPF, pass and filing rules are specific and the penalties sit with the local side. For a small team it's usually more trouble than it's worth to learn from afar.

Does this work for just two or three staff?

Especially for two or three staff. That's exactly the size where building your own payroll function makes no sense.

What if we want our own entity later?

Set it up whenever it makes sense. Many companies start this way, prove the market, then incorporate from a position of knowledge.

Do you handle work passes for foreign staff?

Yes, where the passes apply. We manage the pass and the filings. Always confirm current eligibility and quota with MOM for your case.

How much does it cost to start?

For most employers, near-zero, we run payroll free for the first year. I'll explain how on a quick call, no obligation.
