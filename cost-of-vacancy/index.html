Common questions

How is the cost of a vacancy worked out?
An unfilled role costs more than the unpaid salary. The output that seat would have produced is lost, and the work spills onto other people. We size it as the role’s daily salary × a value multiplier × the days open. You set the multiplier.
Where does the 30% bad-hire figure come from?
It’s a widely cited rule of thumb — once you add the original hiring cost, onboarding, lost productivity and hiring again, a wrong hire tends to land near a third of first-year salary, often more for senior roles.
Is this exact?
No — it’s directional. The point is that an open role is rarely as cheap as it feels, so speed and accuracy in hiring are worth money.
How does JTE bring the cost down?
By shortening time-to-fill and screening for fit so the hire stays. Tell us the role and we’ll give you an honest read.
