What we do

Volume hiring, without the chaos

Hiring at scale is a different discipline from filling one role at a time. A new site coming online, a fresh contract to staff, a division standing up, a seasonal peak bearing down — suddenly you need dozens of the right people by a fixed date, and the process that works for a single hire simply buckles under the numbers. Job ads pull a flood of unscreened applications no one has time to read. Interviews clog a manager’s calendar. Good candidates go cold while you catch up. And the deadline doesn’t move.

This is exactly what a project and mass hiring drive is built for. Instead of stretching your team thin, JTE mobilises a dedicated recruiting effort around your target — one plan, one managed pipeline, one point of coordination — with the capacity to source, screen, interview and onboard people in volume. Whether you need ten hires or a hundred, the work runs to a schedule that’s planned backwards from the day you need everyone at their desks or on the floor.

The hard part isn’t finding bodies — it’s finding the right ones, fast, without the quality quietly slipping as the numbers climb. We’ve run these drives for Singapore employers since 2014, and the discipline that keeps them honest is structure: clear screening standards agreed up front, a steady weekly cadence of qualified candidates, and coordinated interviews and offers that keep momentum from the first week to the last hire. You hit your headcount and your start date — and the hundredth person is screened as carefully as the first.

When you need it

The moments a drive makes sense

Mass hiring isn’t about hiring constantly — it’s about hiring a lot, well, in a defined window. These are the situations we’re built for.

New site & facility openings

A new plant, warehouse, clinic or office has to be fully crewed before it can open — often the whole operational team at once, from the floor up. We staff the site to your commissioning date so day one runs, not stumbles.

Expansions & new divisions

A new contract, a new line of business or a growth push means dozens of hires the existing team can’t absorb. We stand up the headcount in parallel with your own hiring, so the expansion lands on schedule.

Seasonal & peak demand

Peak periods, festive ramps and demand spikes need people fast — and often only for a season. We fill the surge quickly, frequently as contract or temp headcount, and wind it down cleanly when the peak passes.

Project ramp-ups

A major project or shutdown needs a crew assembled for a fixed term with the right trades and certifications. We mobilise the team to the start date and keep the pipeline warm for the inevitable last-minute additions.

How it works

How a volume drive runs

One plan, one team, one pipeline — run to a schedule that’s planned backwards from your deadline.

01

Plan & scope

We map the roles, the numbers, the shift patterns and the go-live date with you — then work backwards into a week-by-week plan. This is also where we agree the screening standards and the right mix of permanent, contract and outsourced headcount.

02

Mobilise the team

A dedicated consultant or pod ramps sourcing immediately — our network built since 2014, targeted advertising, referrals and direct outreach all running in parallel to build the pipeline fast, with one owner accountable for the whole drive.

03

Screen at scale

Every candidate goes through the same structured checks — skills, right to work, availability and attitude — before they reach you. You get a steady weekly flow of qualified people, not a raw dump of applications to sift yourself.

04

Interview & offer

We coordinate interviews, run assessment or walk-in days where volume calls for them, and manage offers and acceptances centrally — so momentum holds and strong candidates don’t drift to a competing offer while you catch up.

05

Onboard the cohort

We coordinate work passes, paperwork and start dates so the whole intake lands together and ready. Where you’ve chosen contract or outsourced headcount, we carry the payroll and compliance from day one.

A realistic word on timing

For frontline and operational roles we can begin sending screened candidates within days of the brief and sustain a steady weekly intake until the numbers are met. Larger or more specialised drives, and any that depend on work passes, take longer — so we build the timeline around your go-live date and share it up front. We’d rather commit to a plan we can hit than a headline number we can’t.

Questions

Common questions

How many hires can you handle at once?–

From ten to a few hundred. We size the recruiting team to your project — a single dedicated consultant for a smaller intake, or a coordinated pod running parallel pipelines for a large ramp. We scope the numbers, the roles and the timeline with you before we mobilise, so the capacity matches the target from day one.

How fast can you fill a large intake?+

We plan backwards from your go-live date. For frontline and operational roles we can begin sending screened candidates within days of the brief and sustain a steady weekly intake until the numbers are met. Timelines depend on the roles, the pass requirements and the market, but we give you a realistic, week-by-week plan at the start rather than an optimistic promise we can’t keep.

How do you keep quality up at volume?+

Scale doesn’t mean sending you everyone with a pulse. We agree clear screening standards up front, then run every candidate through the same structured checks — skills, right to work, availability and attitude — before they reach you. A managed pipeline and a single point of coordination mean the hundredth hire is screened as carefully as the first.

Do you run onsite recruitment or assessment days?+

Yes. For larger drives we can run assessment days, group interviews and onsite or walk-in recruitment events — a fast, structured way to screen and interview many candidates in a short window. We handle the scheduling, the shortlisting and the coordination so your managers meet only the people worth their time.

Can you combine mass hiring with contract or payroll?+

Often the smartest way to ramp. We can place part or all of an intake as contract or temp staff with JTE as employer of record — CPF, leave, IR8A and insurance handled — and convert strong performers to permanent later. We can also run payroll and work-pass administration for the whole cohort, so a large ramp stays compliant and simple.

Which sectors do you cover?+

We run volume drives across engineering, logistics and supply chain, manufacturing, healthcare, and the commercial, support and IT functions that keep those operations running — from frontline and operational roles up to the supervisors and specialists who lead them.

How is a mass-hiring project charged?+

It depends on the mix. Permanent hires within a drive work on a success-fee basis — you pay only when someone starts. Contract or outsourced headcount is charged as a transparent margin on the hourly or monthly rate. For a large, defined project we’ll agree the commercial model clearly with you before any work begins. Get in touch for a quote.
