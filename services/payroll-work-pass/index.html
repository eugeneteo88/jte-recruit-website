What we do

Payroll and work passes, done properly

Payroll looks routine until it isn’t. CPF rates change, someone joins mid-month, a bonus needs pro-rating, a claim comes in late, a foreign employee leaves and needs tax clearance, a work pass is weeks from expiring. Each one is a small thing — but a small error means the wrong number in someone’s account, a late CPF filing, or a lapsed pass that stops an employee from legally working. For the business owner or HR lead already stretched thin, it’s a quiet, recurring source of risk.

We take the whole thing off your desk. JTE has run outsourced payroll and work-pass administration for Singapore employers since 2014 — we calculate pay accurately, file CPF and taxes on time, issue clean payslips, and handle Employment Pass, S Pass and Work Permit applications and renewals through MOM. You keep control and visibility; we carry the admin, the deadlines and the compliance.

It’s built for two kinds of employer: those who simply want the payroll admin off their plate so they can run the business, and those hiring foreign talent who need the work-pass side handled properly alongside the pay. In both cases the promise is the same — your people get paid right, every cycle, and you stop watching the calendar.

Why it matters

Compliance isn’t optional in Singapore

Payroll and work-pass rules here are strict, and the penalties are real. Late or under-paid CPF attracts interest and enforcement. A missed IR21 filing can hold up a departing employee’s final pay and leave you liable for their tax. Employing someone on a lapsed or wrong pass is an offence under the Employment of Foreign Manpower Act — with fines and debarment on the table, not just a warning.

That’s the quiet risk sitting inside every payroll run, and it’s exactly what we’re here to absorb. As a licensed employment agency operating since 2014, staying current with MOM, CPF and IRAS is simply how we work — so the deadlines are met, the filings are right, and your passes never lapse. You get the upside of doing it properly without having to become the expert yourself.

Questions

Common questions

What payroll tasks do you handle?–

The full monthly cycle: gross-to-net calculations, pro-rations for joiners and leavers, overtime, allowances, bonuses, deductions and claims, itemised payslips, salary disbursement, and year-end IR8A preparation. We also maintain leave records and the statutory returns that go with payroll, so the whole run is handled end to end rather than piece by piece.

Do you handle CPF contributions and tax filing (IR8A / IR21)?+

Yes. We calculate and file CPF at the correct rates each month, along with SDL, CDAC, MBMF, SINDA and ECF levies. At year end we prepare and submit IR8A for your employees, and when a foreign employee leaves we handle IR21 tax clearance so their final pay is released correctly.

Can you apply for an Employment Pass, S Pass or Work Permit?+

Yes. We prepare and submit Employment Pass, S Pass and Work Permit applications through MOM, advise on eligibility, salary benchmarks and quota or levy implications before you commit, and manage the process through to issuance and pass collection. It pairs naturally with a permanent or contract hire from overseas.

Do you handle renewals, appeals and dependant passes?+

Yes. We track expiry dates and manage renewals ahead of the deadline, prepare appeals where an application is rejected, and handle Dependant Passes, Long-Term Visit Passes and cancellations when someone joins, moves or leaves. You get a reminder well before a pass lapses, not after.

How is it charged?+

It’s quoted, not a success fee. Pricing depends on your headcount, pay frequency and how much work-pass support you need. Tell us the scope and we’ll give you a straightforward quote. We also run a free payroll pilot so you can see how we work before committing — get in touch for a quote.

Is our payroll data secure and compliant?+

Payroll data is confidential and we treat it that way — access is restricted, records are kept for the statutory retention periods, and every cycle is run against current CPF, IRAS and MOM rules. As a licensed employment agency (MOM EA 14C7215, operating since 2014) compliance is part of how we work, not an afterthought.

Can you take over an existing payroll?+

Yes. We manage the transition from your current provider or in-house setup — migrating employee records, pay history, CPF and pass details — so nothing is missed in the changeover and the first run under JTE lands on time.

Which sectors do you work with?+

We run payroll and work-pass support for employers across engineering, logistics and supply chain, manufacturing, healthcare, the commercial and support functions, and IT and tech — from a handful of staff to a full team of foreign and local employees.
