Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 July 2026

JTE Recruit Pte Ltd ("JTE", "we", "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA). This policy explains what we collect through this website and how we handle it.

Who we are

JTE Recruit Pte Ltd, a licensed employment agency (EA Licence No. 14C7215), 1 Gateway Drive, #10-14 Westgate Tower, Singapore 608531. Email: ask@jte.com.sg.

What we collect

  • Information you give us: when you submit our contact form or email us, we collect the details you provide (such as your name, email address, and message).
  • Usage data: we use Google Analytics, which sets cookies to collect anonymous, aggregated information about how visitors use the site (pages viewed, device, approximate location, referral source). This does not identify you personally.

How we use your data

  • To respond to your enquiries and provide recruitment services.
  • To understand and improve how our website performs.
  • To protect the public against impersonation scams (for example, our number-verification tool).

Cookies

This site uses cookies solely for anonymous analytics via Google Analytics. You can disable cookies in your browser settings; the site will still function normally.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We use Google Analytics (Google LLC) as a service provider for website analytics. We may disclose information where required by law.

Your rights under the PDPA

You may request access to, or correction of, the personal data we hold about you, and you may withdraw consent to our use of your data. To make a request, contact us at ask@jte.com.sg.

Retention

We keep personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes above or as required by law.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be published on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email ask@jte.com.sg.
