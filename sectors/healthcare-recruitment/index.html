Questions

Common questions

What healthcare roles does JTE recruit for in Singapore?–

Across clinical, allied health, care and support, and healthcare administration — from clinic and patient-facing roles to operations and management, on permanent and contract terms.

Do you recruit for clinics and healthcare employers?+

Yes. We recruit on behalf of healthcare employers — clinics, providers and support organisations — not for individual job seekers.

Do you handle permanent and contract healthcare roles?+

Yes, including permanent hires and contract or locum-style cover, with payroll handled where needed.

How much does healthcare recruitment cost?+

Permanent placements work on a success-fee basis — you only pay when you hire. We agree the terms clearly with you before any search begins. Get in touch for a quote — and see current healthcare salary ranges in Singapore to benchmark a package.

Can you help with work passes for foreign healthcare staff?+

Yes. We can manage work-permit, S Pass and Employment Pass applications and ongoing payroll, so onboarding stays compliant and simple.

Need to hire in healthcare?

Tell us the role and the setting. No obligations — just a straight conversation with recruiters who take care seriously.
