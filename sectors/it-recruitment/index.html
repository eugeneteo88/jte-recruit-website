Questions

Common questions

Do you place permanent and contract IT staff?–

Yes. We handle permanent IT hires as well as contract and project-based tech roles. Permanent placements are success-fee — no hire, no fee — while contract engagements can run with the contractor employed and payrolled by JTE.

Can the IT contractor be on your payroll?+

Yes. Under our contract-staffing model, JTE acts as the employer of record: the contractor sits on JTE’s payroll and we handle CPF, leave, IR8A and insurance. You simply direct the work. It’s ideal for project-based or flexible tech headcount.

Do you handle IT payroll outsourcing?+

Yes. If you already have your tech staff but want the admin off your plate, we can run payroll and compliance for your IT team — CPF, leave, IR8A, payslips and statutory filings — as an outsourced service.

Can you help with Employment Pass or S Pass for foreign tech talent?+

Yes. Alongside the hire, we can manage Employment Pass, S Pass and work-permit applications and ongoing payroll, so onboarding an overseas IT professional stays compliant and simple.

What IT roles do you cover?+

Our core is the practical, high-volume roles Singapore employers hire most — from IT support (L1/L2) and helpdesk through system, software and application engineers, infrastructure and networks. We also go up to senior and specialist engineers, cybersecurity, cloud and DevOps, ERP and business systems, and AI/data roles.

How do you charge for IT recruitment?+

Permanent placements work on a success-fee basis — you only pay when you hire. Contract staffing and payroll outsourcing are quoted based on scope and headcount. We agree the terms clearly with you before any work begins — and see current IT & tech salary ranges in Singapore to benchmark a package.

Are you a licensed recruitment agency?+

Yes. JTE Recruit Pte Ltd holds MOM Employment Agency Licence 14C7215 and has been hiring for Singapore employers since 2014.

We also run engineering recruitment, commercial & support recruitment and healthcare recruitment in Singapore — across permanent and contract staffing hires.
