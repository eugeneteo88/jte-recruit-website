Good to Know

Employer FAQ

How does JTE charge employers to hire?

Permanent placements work on a success-fee basis — you only pay when you hire. We agree the terms with you clearly before any search begins, so there are no surprises. Contract, outsourced and project hiring are quoted to suit the assignment. Get in touch for a quote.

What industries does JTE recruit for?

We have particular strength in engineering, logistics and supply chain, manufacturing, and healthcare recruitment, along with the commercial and support functions that keep those businesses running. We also place talent across other sectors in Singapore.

How quickly can you fill a role?

It depends on the role, but we move quickly without cutting corners. For urgent contract needs we can often mobilise pre-vetted people within days. After we understand your brief, we give you a realistic timeline upfront.

Do you guarantee your placements?

Yes. Permanent placements come with a replacement guarantee. The length depends on the seniority and type of role, and we agree it with you before the search starts.

Can JTE handle work passes and payroll for foreign hires?

Yes. Work pass application and payroll administration are among our services, so we can manage the paperwork and compliance for foreign employees on your behalf.

Is JTE a licensed recruitment agency in Singapore?

Yes. JTE Recruit Pte Ltd holds Employment Agency Licence No. 14C7215, issued by Singapore's Ministry of Manpower, and has been hiring for Singapore employers since 2014.
