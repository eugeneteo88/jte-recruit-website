Common questions

Straight answers

Do I have to pay a placement fee to get grant help?–

No. If you’ve found your own person or you’re reskilling existing staff, we’ll just handle the grant application for a flat fee. The placement fee only applies if you want us to find the candidate too — and in that case, the grant application is included.

What does the application help cost?+

A flat fee, agreed upfront — for a typical workforce grant it starts around $1,500. We don’t take a cut of your grant. And if you end up hiring the candidate through us, the application fee is waived.

How can you get me more support than a grant consultant?+

The support level often depends on who you hire — for example, a Singaporean aged 40+ or someone unemployed for six months or more unlocks the enhanced 90% CCP rate. A consultant can only work with the person you’ve already picked. We can bring you the profile that qualifies for the most support in the first place.

Which grants can you actually apply for on my behalf?+

The ones that need a real application — CCP, Mid-Career Pathways, Part-Time Re-employment and Job Redesign. The wage credits (senior, disability, ex-offender) are paid automatically by IRAS; there’s nothing to apply for, so we simply make sure you’re set up to receive them.

Are you a government agency or official partner?+

No — we’re a recruitment agency acting on your behalf, the same way a grant consultant does. The grants themselves are run by WSG (moving to the new SWDA agency) and IRAS.
