JTE will never ask you for money

Legitimate recruitment is always free for job seekers. If anyone claiming to be from JTE asks you to pay, deposit, or complete paid tasks, it's a scam. Stop, and verify the number above.

How the scam works

1

The approach

You get an unsolicited WhatsApp from someone using a JTE consultant’s name or photo, offering an easy, high-paying work-from-home job.

2

The hook

You’re asked to complete simple “tasks” and make a small deposit. They let you withdraw a little early, so it feels real and trustworthy.

3

The trap

To “unlock” bigger earnings you’re pushed to deposit more and more. Eventually the money vanishes, and so does the scammer.

Warning signs of a scam

  • ×Asks you to pay a deposit, “top-up”, or complete paid tasks to earn or withdraw money.
  • ×Offers unusually high pay for simple work-from-home “tasks” (liking videos, boosting orders, etc.).
  • ×Lets you withdraw a small amount early to build trust, then pressures you to deposit more.
  • ×Contacts you out of the blue for a job you never applied for.
  • ×Uses a consultant’s photo or name but a number you can’t verify here.

JTE will never

  • ✓Ask you for money: deposits, fees, or “task top-ups”.
  • ✓Ask you to complete paid online tasks to earn commissions.
  • ✓Recruit you via random WhatsApp or Telegram messages for jobs you didn’t apply for.
  • ✓Request your bank login, OTP, or crypto wallet details.

Think you’ve been targeted?

If you’ve received a suspicious message or lost money, act quickly. In Singapore you can:

  • ✓Call the ScamShield Helpline at 1799, or the Police Anti-Scam Hotline at 1800-255-0000.
  • ✓Report scams at scamshield.gov.sg.
  • ✓Block and report the number inside WhatsApp, and tell your bank if you sent money.
  • ✓Let us know at ask@jte.com.sg so we can warn others.
