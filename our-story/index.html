How it began

Built by recruiters, for employers

JTE Recruit opened its doors in 2014 with a simple belief: hiring is too important to be transactional. Too many agencies blast out CVs and hope something sticks. We wanted to do it properly — understand the business, understand the people, and make matches that actually last.

More than a decade on, that hasn’t changed. We’re a MOM-licensed employment agency based at Westgate in Jurong East, working with employers across engineering, logistics, manufacturing, healthcare and the commercial roles that keep them running.

The founders

Three people, one company

JTE Recruit was founded in 2014 by Eugene and Jonathan — two recruiters who believed hiring could be done with more honesty, and a lot more personality. In 2015, Adaline joined as one of our earliest hires. She quickly became one of our strongest performers and helped build JTE into what it is today — and along the way, she became a partner.

Today the three of us are the pillars of the company — still hands-on, still doing the work we love.

The people

Real personality, real mileage

Between us we bring more than 50 years of combined experience. Some of us have been in the trade for over a decade; all of us genuinely enjoy it. That mix of hard-won expertise and actual personality is what clients remember — and why they keep coming back.

We know our sectors well enough to judge a candidate properly, not just keyword-match a CV. And we’re the kind of people you won’t mind picking up the phone to.

What we believe

The way we work

Fit over filler

We send you people who fit — screened for how they work, not just what’s on paper. A shortlist we’d vouch for, never a stack of CVs to sift.

Employers first

Companies are who we work for. That means straight timelines, honest updates, and never wasting your time to look busy.

Honest, always

Real advice on salary, the market and what’s realistic — even when it isn’t what you were hoping to hear. It’s how trust gets built.

We never charge candidates

Genuine recruitment is paid by employers, never job seekers. We’ll also help you spot the scams that pretend otherwise — it matters to us.

Let’s find your next great hire

Whether it’s one key person or a hundred, we’d love to hear what you’re building. No obligations — just a straight conversation.
