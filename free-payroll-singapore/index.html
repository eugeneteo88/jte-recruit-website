Why it matters

Getting payroll wrong in Singapore is costly

Payroll and work-pass rules here are strict, and the penalties are real. Late or under-paid CPF attracts interest and enforcement. A missed IR21 filing can hold up a departing employee’s final pay. Employing someone on a lapsed or wrong pass is an offence under the Employment of Foreign Manpower Act — fines and debarment, not just a warning.

That’s the quiet risk inside every payroll run — and exactly what we absorb. As a licensed employment agency operating since 2014, staying current with MOM, CPF and IRAS is simply how we work. You get it done properly without having to become the expert yourself.

There isn’t one beyond that single session. The service is funded by our insurance partners, so we don’t need to charge you for the payroll. You’re under no obligation to buy any insurance — the session is a no-pressure review of your team’s coverage.

No. Your team can sit under JTE’s entity — we act as the employer of record, running payroll and compliance while you direct the work. That makes this ideal if you’re a startup or an overseas company that doesn’t yet have a Singapore entity.
