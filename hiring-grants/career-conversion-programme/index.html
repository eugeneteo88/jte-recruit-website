How it works

Two ways to use CCP

CCP helps you build the team you can’t hire off-the-shelf — by funding the gap while a capable person learns the role. There are two routes, and the first is where a recruiter matters most.

Place-and-Train

Hire someone new, then reskill

You bring in a mid-career hire from outside — often from an adjacent industry — and train them into the role on the job. This is the route we run: we source and vet the candidate, you get the salary support. Ideal when the perfect “ready-made” hire doesn’t exist in the market.

Recruiter-led · we find the person
Job Redesign Reskilling

Reskill people you already employ

Existing staff are retrained for a redesigned or higher-value role as your business shifts. No new hire needed — the support offsets the reskilling of your current team. We can advise on structuring this, though it sits inside your own workforce.

Common questions

CCP, answered plainly

How much of the salary does the government actually cover?–

Up to 70% of monthly salary (capped at $4,000/month) for a standard mid-career hire, and up to 90% (capped at $6,000/month) if they’re a Singaporean aged 40 and above or have been unemployed for six months or longer. Training costs can be subsidised up to 90% on top.

Do I have to hire someone new, or can I reskill my own staff?+

Both. “Place-and-Train” is for a new external hire — that’s where we come in, sourcing and vetting the candidate. “Job Redesign Reskilling” is for retraining people you already employ into a redesigned role.

Does the hire need any particular background?+

They should be a Singapore Citizen or PR moving into a role that’s genuinely new to them — CCP is designed for career switchers. They don’t need prior experience in the field; that’s the point of the reskilling.

What does JTE do that a grant consultant doesn’t?+

A consultant helps with the application. We find you the person first — a vetted, capable mid-career candidate — and then help structure the hire so it qualifies. Recruiting is the hard part; the grant makes it cheaper. See our full hiring-grants hub for the other schemes.

What does it cost to work with JTE on this?+

Our recruiting works on a success-fee basis — you only pay when you hire. We agree the terms clearly upfront, and the CCP salary support offsets a large part of the new hire’s cost during reskilling. Benchmark a package against our Singapore Salary Guide.
