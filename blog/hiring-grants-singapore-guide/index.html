The employer’s guide to hiring grants in Singapore

Which grant, for which hire — the supports worth knowing, in plain English.

Eugene Teo

Co-Founder, JTE Recruit · 21 August 2026 · 4 min read

Singapore gives employers a surprising amount of help to hire and train people. The problem is it’s scattered across agencies, buried in jargon, and most business owners never get a clear map of it. So here’s the map — plainly, and in one place.

The easiest way to think about it: there are two kinds of hiring grant. The ones you apply for — these are bigger, tied to a specific hire, and can really change your maths. And the ones that just happen automatically — the government tops up your wage bill through IRAS, no forms needed. Know both, and you stop leaving money on the table.

The ones you apply for

These are the ones worth real attention, because they’re tied to a decision you’re already making — a hire.

Career Conversion Programme (CCP)

Hire someone who doesn’t have the exact experience — a genuine career-switcher — and the government covers up to 70% of their salary while you train them, up to 90% if they’re 40 and above or have been out of work a while. It’s the big one. Here’s how CCP actually works, and the CCP page has a calculator.

Mid-Career Pathways Programme (MCPP)

Not ready to commit to a mature hire? MCPP lets you trial someone 40 and above on a four-to-six-month attachment first, with most of their allowance funded — then hire them if it works out. The try-before-you-hire grant, explained.

The ones that just happen

These you don’t apply for at all. Based on your CPF records, the government pays them straight to you — wage top-ups for giving lower-wage staff a raise, for employing seniors, people with disabilities, or ex-offenders. Small individually, but real, and easy to overlook. The automatic wage credits, in one place.

Which one, for which hire

  • Hiring a career-switcher into a new role — that’s CCP.
  • Want to trial a 40-plus hire before committing — that’s MCPP.
  • Giving a lower-wage worker a raise, or hiring a senior / person with a disability / ex-offender — the automatic credits quietly kick in.
