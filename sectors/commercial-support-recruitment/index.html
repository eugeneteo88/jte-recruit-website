Questions

Common questions

What commercial and support roles does JTE recruit for?–

Across accounting & finance, HR, admin & office support, sales & business development, customer service, marketing, procurement and executive support — from executives to management, on permanent and contract terms.

Do you recruit permanent and contract support roles?+

Yes — permanent hires as well as contract, temp and maternity cover. We can also employ and payroll contract staff on your behalf.

Do you cover both junior and senior roles?+

Yes — from coordinators and executives up to finance, HR and operations managers.

How much does commercial recruitment cost?+

Permanent placements work on a success-fee basis — you only pay when you hire. We agree the terms clearly with you before any search begins. Get in touch for a quote.

Can you help with work passes for foreign hires?+

Yes. We can manage Employment Pass, S Pass and work-permit applications and ongoing payroll, so onboarding stays compliant and simple.
